# 1.8 Outline

The remainder of this PhD dissertation is arranged as follows:

Chapter 2 presents the work done around the publishing of bicycle parking infrastructure data, which comprises three different peer-reviewed publications. The work done in this chapter relates to RQ1, RQ2 and RQ3.

Chapter 3 focuses on the public transport route planning use case and presents 2 papers around this topic. This chapter is mainly devoted to the study of RQ2.

Chapter 4 describes the reference architecture related to the publishing of railway infrastructure data and the RCC use case, comprising one paper on this topic. The work of this chapter is mainly related to RQ1 and RQ3.

Lastly, in Chapter 5 the main conclusions of this PhD work are discussed, together with a set of reflections regarding potential future work.
